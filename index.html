01About

Started in ML. Now shipping AI.

I'm an AI engineer who started in ML. My first serious work was graph-neural-network research at IIT Gandhinagar; for the last 3+ years I've shipped AI to production at Argusoft: LangGraph agents that watch 10+ e-commerce platforms, RAG over 1,000+ scanned documents, and voice agents that sit in Zoom calls and capture what was decided.

Lately I'm obsessed with the part most demos skip: knowing when the model is wrong. My hackathon work, Cascade and Nexus, is built around falsifiers, deterministic checks, and agents that abstain instead of guessing. On the side, I write expert-level evaluation tasks for frontier LLMs, and I keep returning to my research roots: ML for weather, climate and air quality.

04Research

Graph neural networks for the air we breathe.

Spatiotemporal forecasting of PM2.5 at IIT Gandhinagar, under Prof. Nipun Batra with PhD mentor Zeel Patel. Where my head is today: ML for weather, climate and atmospheric modelling.

Workshop on Climate Change and ML · IITGN @ Amalthea 2022

Graph Neural Network for Air Quality Index Forecasting

Forecasting PM2.5 across 184 cities in Central China from four years of 3-hourly data and 17 meteorological features, with geography and wind transport built into the graph itself.

Cities in the graph
184
Meteorological features
17
Architectures benchmarked
7
RMSE, PM2.5-GNN replication
~19.6
01

Temporal beats spatial.

Ablations on distance threshold, correlation threshold and history length showed time matters more than space for short-range AQI forecasts.

02

The boundary layer matters most.

Boundary layer height and surface pressure were the two most predictive features, consistent with the PM2.5-GNN paper.

03

Physics in the graph.

300 km distance and 1,200 m mountain-barrier constraints, with wind speed, advection and transport direction on every edge.

Benchmarked MLP · LSTM · GRU · GC-LSTM · nodesFCGRU · PM2.5-GNN on RMSE · MAE · CSI · POD · FAR
